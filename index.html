HOW IT WORKS

Three small steps.
A clearer routine.

STEP 01

Choose your focus

Pick one or two habits you'd like to build, such as regular movement, a steadier wind-down routine, or taking time to reset.

STEP 02

Check in as you go

Mark a habit when it fits your day. Missed a day? No guilt and no starting over. Just continue when you're ready.

STEP 03

Reflect each week

Review what felt helpful, adjust your goals, and make a plan that feels realistic for the week ahead.

GOOD TO KNOW

Designed with care.

Is Orbit a medical service?

No. This concept is for general wellness habits and self-reflection. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent disease and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Does this website save my health information?

This demo does not create an account or send your entries to a server. Avoid entering sensitive personal or health information. A production service would need a clear privacy notice and secure data handling before launch.

Can I use it on my phone?

The concept site is responsive and designed to work in desktop and mobile browsers. A full account, cloud sync, reminders, and paid membership still need to be built.
